# Community Code of Conduct

Banna is committed to a welcoming, safe, and technically constructive community
for contributors and users of every background and experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction and taking responsibility for
  impact.
- Keep reviews focused on the project, its users, and verifiable technical
  outcomes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method
on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive
report. Include links or screenshots when possible.

The maintainer may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently exclude participants when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is a
violation of this policy.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where a person is
representing Banna.
